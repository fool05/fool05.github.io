# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors to fool's public personal website, reading Haoyu Yang's background, education, technical interests, awards, and contact links in Chinese or English. The existing README, content files, and completed personal-information questionnaire establish this purpose.

## Product Purpose

Present the user's real academic background and current interests in AI Infrastructure and KV Cache. Keep the PRISM homepage and its education and awards pages easy to browse. The site is published on GitHub Pages.

## Brand Personality

Personal, bookish, quietly mysterious. The user requested a light touch of the atmosphere and elements of Lord of the Mysteries, and supplied the existing Fool illustration as their avatar.

## Anti-references

The user's request is for a touch of atmosphere on the existing personal site, so extensive fictional content, a game-like interface, or a wholesale structural redesign would exceed this scope.

## Design Principles

- Keep the user's biography, dates, awards, and contact information intact.
- Let the supplied avatar carry the strongest visual character.
- Use decoration to frame content while keeping reading and navigation straightforward.
- Keep Chinese and English equally complete.
- Preserve theme switching, responsive layout, and the existing publishing workflow.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No additional personal accessibility requirements were supplied. Maintain readable contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic navigation, mobile layout, and reduced-motion support as implementation standards.
